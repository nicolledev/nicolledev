### My journey
My journey in technology began at ETEC Aristóteles Ferreira, where I studied Systems Development. I chose this path because I believe technology can do more than create new tools: it can transform ideas into solutions and make a meaningful difference in people’s lives.
Like any new journey, mine came with challenges. I had to keep learning, adapt when things felt difficult, and trust that each step was helping me grow. Those experiences strengthened my determination and made me even more curious about how technology works and how it can solve real problems.
This year, I decided to take a more intentional step toward my professional goals by beginning a technical program in IT at Senac Santos. I’m continuing to build my knowledge through practical projects and explore the areas that inspire me most: software development and data.
I’m still at the beginning of my journey, but I’m excited to keep learning, creating, and growing in tech—one challenge and one project at a time.
